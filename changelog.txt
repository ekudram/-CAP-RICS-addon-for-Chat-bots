==========
1.0.0
==========
Optional addon for [CAP] RICS. Load after RICS (release or DEV/Beta).

Moderator-only bot commands (JSON for an AI chatbot, not public chat):
- !botstate — colony snapshot
- !botpawns — free colonists
- !botpawncheck — hediffs / rest vs healer-serum advice
- !botthreats — hostiles, manhunters, scaria, fires
- !botmap — map, biome, weather
- !botwealth — wealth breakdown
- !botresources — stockpile counts
- !botgear — assigned pawn loadout and weapon stats
- !botfaction — faction / Ideology
- !botowned — RICS-owned gear anywhere
- !botdisown — unclaim one owned item
